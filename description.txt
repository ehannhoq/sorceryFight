Sorcery Fight is heavily inspired by anime/manga Jujutsu Kaisen.

The mod adds:
Seven innate techniques, Limitless, Shrine, Private Pure Love Train, Vessel, the 'Physically Gifted' Heavenly Restriction, Blood Manipulation, and Star Rage,
as well as 2 custom boss domains!
